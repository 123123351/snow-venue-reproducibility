Reproducibility Package (v3)

Anonymised code and data supporting the study on the life-cycle carbon of indoor snow venues across climate zones: a hot-summer warm-winter (HSWW) main case (Guangzhou) with a severe-cold comparator (Harbin). All author-identifying information has been removed.

Contents

code/run_pipeline.py : the single computation script. It contains the monthly forward physics model (six end uses, intensity-based snow making), the expenditure-derived electricity anchor with an unallocated anchor residual, Monte Carlo (10,000), Sobol global sensitivity, the XGBoost/SHAP surrogate (5,000 Latin-hypercube evaluations, 80/20 split), the climate gradient and the mitigation scenarios.
data/ : machine-readable authoritative outputs (results.json, robustness results, validation, comparator LCA and supporting inputs).
weather/ : typical-year EPW files for the two venues, built from Open-Meteo ERA5 reanalysis.
energyplus/ : EnergyPlus IDF and hourly CSV for six cases, provided as evidence for the independent cross-engine check. No scripts to run EnergyPlus are included; EnergyPlus is a separate verification engine and does not produce the reported totals.
figures/ : the eight manuscript figures and the figure source-data workbook. No figure-generation scripts are included.
Reproduction

Python 3 with numpy, scipy, SALib, xgboost, shap, matplotlib and openpyxl.
From code/, run python run_pipeline.py. It regenerates results.json (baseline LCA, disaggregation, Monte Carlo, Sobol, SHAP, scenarios and grid decarbonization) and the internal fig_climate_response.png; the regenerated numbers match data/results.json.
Every sampler uses a fixed seed: the Sobol sample uses seed 2026, the nine climate positions use distinct seeds 2026-2034, and the Monte Carlo, SHAP and scenario functions use dedicated local generators, so results are independent of call order and identical on repeated runs.
What is produced independently (not regenerated by run_pipeline.py)

data/robustness_results.json : Dirichlet end-use perturbation, Iman-Conover correlation and equipment tests, from a separate robustness computation.
data/ep_validation.json and ep_holdout.json : the independent EnergyPlus cross-engine validation and holdout (EnergyPlus 25.1; the IDF and hourly CSV evidence is in energyplus/).
data/current_shares.json, harbin_lca_current.json, equipment_embodied.json and mech_cooling_hours.json : independently compiled supporting data.
The eight manuscript figures and the source-data workbook in figures/ : produced by a separate figure workflow. The 90% bootstrap confidence intervals drawn on Figure 4(b) are real (1,000 resamples of the held-out test rows); their lower and upper bounds are tabulated in the Fig4 sheet of the workbook.
Scope notes

The electricity anchor is expenditure-derived (a reported electricity cost divided by an assumed tariff), not a meter reading. The part the forward model does not resolve is an unallocated anchor residual and is not redistributed.
The EnergyPlus verification covers envelope, infiltration and latent loads; its floor is adiabatic and internal gains are omitted, whereas the forward model retains floor and internal-load terms.
Steel quantities are officially reported; concrete and glass quantities are estimates and are flagged in the warnings.
The end-of-life term applies a uniform net ratio as a stated simplification, and construction energy is included in the material stage.
